# Route Difficulty & Economy

**Route Difficulty** Each route has a difficulty from 1 to 10, equal to the higher tier of its two stations. Level 1 can be done with the starting ship, though not safely. Any route can be attempted; the [danger rating](../ship/ship-rating.md) shows how far it outclasses your ship.

**Pay per unit:** 1.6 × good base price × 1.55^(d−1) × random 0.85–1.2 × (1 + distance/300).

Better goods unlock at higher difficulties:

| Difficulty | Goods |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Ice Water, Hydroponic Greens, Textiles, Machine Parts |
| 2 | Medical Supplies, Ore Concentrate |
| 3 | Fusion Cells, Cryo Colonists |
| 4 | Nanofiber, Terraforming Seeds |
| 5 | Quantum Chips, Luxury Goods |
| 6 | Antimatter Pods, Xeno Artifacts |
| 7 | Dark Matter |
| 8 | Neural Cores |
| 9 | Singularity Shards |
| 10 | Void Relics |

A route of difficulty d offers goods unlocked at d, d−1 or d−2. The route to the Terminus carries the special cargo **KL9 Shutdown Code** (see [Story in the Game](../story.md)).

With this tuning, reaching each new rating takes roughly 2–3 deliveries.
