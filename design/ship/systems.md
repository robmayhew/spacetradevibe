# Systems

Upgrade cost = base cost × 1.6 for each level already bought.

| System | Counts toward rating | Start → Max | Effect | Base cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weapons Core | Yes | 1 → 10 | All weapon damage ×1.75 per level | 210 |
| Hull Plating | Yes | 1 → 10 | 120 hull, ×1.43 per level | 180 |
| Deflector Shield | Yes | 0 → 10 | 35 shield at level 1, ×1.43 per level. Recharges 22%/s after 2.5 s without being hit | 240 |
| Engines | No | 1 → 6 | Speed 40, +6 per level | 300 |
| Cargo Hold | No | 1 → 10 | 8 units, +4 per level (more pay per contract) | 250 |

**Repairs:** hull is repaired at a station. A full repair costs 50 cr × 1.55^(Hull level−1), scaled by how much hull is missing.

## Visuals

The ship shows battle damage, and as it's upgraded it shows new weapons, better hull plating, engines and more. What you see in combat and docking is your actual loadout.

| Upgrade | On the ship |
| --- | --- |
| Engines | One nozzle; two from level 3; three from level 5. Flames grow with level and burn blue-white from level 5. |
| Hull Plating | Extra armor plates at levels 3, 5, 7 and 9 |
| Deflector Shield | Glowing emitters on the wingtips, brighter each level |
| Weapons Core | A power line down the spine, brighter each level |
| Cargo Hold | Cargo pods behind the canopy: 1 at level 3, up to 4 at level 10 |
| Weapons | Nose gun (Pulse Laser), twin stub barrels (Scatter Cannon), wing missile pods that show remaining shots (Seeker Missiles), and beam rails along the canopy (Ion Beam) |

**Battle damage** grows as hull drops:

- From 15% damage, scorch marks start to appear, spreading across hull, plating and mounts; the paint darkens.
- From 25%, sparks fly off the damaged spots.
- From 50%, a wing catches fire and trails embers.
- From 75%, the engines sputter.
